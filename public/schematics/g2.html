Polygon lifecycle — from a walked boundary to a Location ID

§6 approval gate · the geometry must clear four checks before an identity is issued

A Location ID is an assertion that this piece of ground is distinct, is what the land record says it is, and is not already claimed. The four checks establish exactly that, and the identity is issued only once they clear. Both the raw track and the simplified working polygon are retained: the first is evidence, the second is the geometry the system operates on, and conflating them would mean either slow checks or a disputed boundary with no original to appeal to.
