Deployment, Hosting & Data Ownership

The ownership position is unambiguous: KSLSA owns the programme record in full; Grobet India Agrotech hosts and operates the platform as Technology Partner; the data is resident in India; and KSLSA may export the complete record — including the audit log — in open formats, at any time and on exit. Departmental and remote-sensing sources feed the platform through read-only machine interfaces: the platform reads, and does not write into, departmental systems of record.
