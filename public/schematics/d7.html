Diagram 7 · Flow A — Land identification to planting approvalSpecification §8.1 · states per §6

Land enters the programme through a gate, not a form. The department identifies and surveys; the verification cadre's field decision is blocking; a named custodian must accept the post-plantation obligation before approval is issued. Only then is the Location ID minted and the tag issued. Rejection is a first-class outcome — it returns to the department with structured reasons, and the rejection history stays on the parcel permanently.
