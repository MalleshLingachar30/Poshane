DIAGRAM 5 · SPECIFICATION §6

Land Parcel Lifecycle & the Approval Gate

The central state machine of the platform, enforcing principle P1. States 1–8 constitute the approval gate: the platform makes it structurally impossible to record a planting event against a parcel that has not reached state 8 — enforced in the domain service, not at the screen. Rejection is a first-class outcome with structured reasons, and a permanently attached history.
