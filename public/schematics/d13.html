Audit Log & the Evidential Chain

Every state transition in the platform writes an immutable, append-only entry carrying six dimensions: what changed, who changed it, when, where, how, and why. Entries are never updated or deleted — a correction is a new entry that supersedes an earlier one, with the superseded entry still readable. Below, the record structure; beneath it, the chain of evidence that answers an external auditor without relying on anything a person merely typed into a box.
