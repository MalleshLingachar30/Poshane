Conceptual Data Model — Parcel and Location ID as the Spine

The principal entities and their relationships, grouped by domain. Everything joins back to the spine at the centre: one Parcel holds exactly one Location ID; one Location ID is served by many physical Tag Instances over time; one Parcel receives many Batches. Survival Census is written against Parcel + Batch and Audit against Parcel; authority flows from Identity through Role Assignment to Scope; and an Audit Log Entry can attach to any entity in the model. This is the conceptual model, not a schema.
