What the imagery can and cannot resolve

The limits stated plainly, so no expectation is set that the system cannot meet

Resolution, revisit frequency and cost form a triangle in which only two can be had at once. What the programme needs is not sharpness but a consistent, comparable time series over the same polygon, which is a frequency problem — and the free tier solves it. Stating the limits in the specification is deliberate: an expectation that satellites will verify first-year survival, once formed in a meeting, is far more damaging than the limitation itself.
