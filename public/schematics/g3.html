The overlap gate — three verdicts from one check

§6.3 · thresholds held in reference data, tunable by KSLSA without redeployment

Overlap cannot be a yes or no. Consumer handsets are accurate to three to five metres, so two officers walking a genuinely shared boundary will produce polygons that intersect slightly, every time — reject on any intersection and the cadre will start gaming the walk. The check therefore classifies: slivers are tolerated and logged, material overlaps go to a human at district level, and near-identity is blocked outright. Percentages are always taken against the smaller parcel, because a small duplicate inside a large parent is invisible any other way.
