One parcel, end to end — what is owned and what is rented

Every step in the life of a single parcel, and which of them depend on a commercial supplier

The question a funder or an auditor asks is not which vendors appear anywhere in the system, but which of them the record depends on. Parcel geometry, the overlap gate, area reconciliation and the stored survival and canopy figures all run inside infrastructure in the India region. Commercial dependence is confined to two steps, both of which only affect what an officer looks at, never what the platform holds.
