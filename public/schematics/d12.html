Diagram 12 · Offline-first field operationSpecification §11

Connectivity across 240 taluks cannot be assumed. Field capture is designed as if the network does not exist: the device carries its reference data, capture runs fully offline, and every payload is signed, encrypted and queued in order on the device itself. When connectivity returns, the sync service verifies, orders by device sequence, and commits. Capture never blocks on network — a field officer in a village without coverage is never stopped from working.
