Planting is recorded against the tag as a batch; survival is a measured, tag-anchored, photo-backed observation — not a submitted number. The agency that plants cannot alone record the authoritative survival census: an independent audit-cadre officer must co-sign it. A census below threshold raises casualty replacement as a new Batch ID against the same Location ID, and generates a demand signal back to the nursery.
